Local notification enhancement

微信通知进化

An independent tool that listens to WeChat notifications and republishes them as Android MessagingStyle conversations.

Download ↓

Core features

  • Conversation-style aggregation for multiple messages
  • Private, group and service-account notification channels
  • Uses original WeChat content intent when opened
  • Android Auto conversation metadata and compatible actions
  • Privacy-safe diagnostics and optional keep-alive
  • Optional bubbles and learned public Launcher entry

Good for

Richer conversation notifications, supported direct actions, Android Auto presentation, notification diagnostics and optional bubbles.

Privacy & data boundary

Notification access is required. Diagnostic reports exclude message text, contacts, Main_User, keys, extras and credentials. Recent processing events are memory-only. Simulated reply text is not logged, persisted or uploaded. Stable conversation IDs use a private random salt and HMAC.

Known limits

Simulated replies never reach WeChat; simulated read only clears local state. Bubble, direct-chat and background reliability depend on Android, launcher, vendor policy and WeChat. Notification history is not a complete chat backup. The app is independent of and not endorsed by Tencent.
