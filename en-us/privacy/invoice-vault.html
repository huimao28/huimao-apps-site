InvoiceVault Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

InvoiceVault is a local-first personal invoice archive. This policy explains the data the app actually handles and its optional data flows.

Data handled

The app may process imported originals and invoice number, code, date, buyer/seller names and tax IDs, item description, amount, tax, total, raw recognized text, category, status, filename, MIME type, hashes, recognition times, and errors. Invoices may also contain names, companies, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or bank details.

Local storage and import

You import PDF, image, or e-invoice XML files through Android’s document picker. Originals are copied to the app-private directory and structured records to a private Room database. PDFs use their text layer first and, when needed, on-device ML Kit Chinese text recognition; XML is parsed locally. There is no app account, and invoices are not uploaded by default.

Recognition and classification

OCR, text-layer and field parsing depend on layout and scan quality, and classification may be wrong. Manually verify important fields. InvoiceVault is not a tax, accounting, or reimbursement-compliance service.

Export, viewing, and sharing

At your request the app can create CSV output or a complete ZIP containing the database and originals. Android FileProvider can grant a selected app temporary read access to an original or archive. Recipients and chosen storage locations process copies under their own policies.

WebDAV backup

The app contacts WebDAV only after you configure a server and actively test, back up, list, restore, or delete. Server URL, username, password, and directory are stored encrypted with AES-GCM using a key protected by Android Keystore. The uploaded ZIP contains the Room database and user originals; its contents are not separately end-to-end encrypted, so prefer HTTPS. Your provider receives credentials, network address, files, and request metadata under its policy.

Permissions and system components

The manifest declares only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for user-initiated WebDAV. Import uses the system document picker without broad storage access; sharing uses a non-exported FileProvider and temporary read grants. Components include Android/Jetpack, Room, PDFBox Android, Google ML Kit Text Recognition, OkHttp, and Gson. The picker, share targets, and your WebDAV provider are external components.

Retention, deletion, and uninstall

Records and originals remain until you delete them in the app or clear app data. Deleting a record can remove its private original. Exports, shared copies, and remote backups must be deleted separately where stored. Uninstalling normally permanently removes the private database, originals, and WebDAV credentials; export first if needed. Restoring a backup may overwrite local data.

Children

The app is for personal document management and is not designed specifically for children. Guardians should prevent children from importing sensitive documents about themselves or others.

Changes and contact

Material changes will update the date on this page. For questions or privacy requests, contact Huimao through the project repository. Never attach real invoices, tax IDs, account numbers, email addresses, or WebDAV credentials to a public issue.
