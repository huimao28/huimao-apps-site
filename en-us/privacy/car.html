Huimao Vehicle Manager
Privacy

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Data processed

The app stores the information you enter and the settings and records created by its features locally on your device. The developer does not operate a central server that receives this content.

Vehicle profile, mileage, maintenance, fuel or charging, costs, trips, OBD adapter identifiers, live readings and fault codes are local. Optional Google Drive or WebDAV backups contain the records you choose.

2. Permissions and purpose

Only permissions needed for the selected feature are requested. Depending on the app, these can include location, notifications, network access, Bluetooth or notification access. You can revoke them in Android settings.

3. Third-party services

Optional features may connect to Google Play, Baidu Maps, Google Drive, WebDAV, AI providers, or links you explicitly open. Their own terms and privacy policies apply. Data is sent only when the related feature is used.

4. Sharing, retention and security

There are no third-party ads and personal data is not sold or rented. Local data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Optional cloud data follows the chosen provider’s retention rules. Credentials use Android security facilities where available, but no storage or transmission is absolutely secure.

5. Your choices and contact

You control permissions, optional history, exports, backups and connected services. The app is not directed at children. For questions, contact the developer through the relevant GitHub repository and never post credentials or private records in a public issue.
