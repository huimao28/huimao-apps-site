Local-first personal invoice archive

发票匣 / InvoiceVault

An Android tool for importing, recognizing, searching and backing up personal invoices.

Transferir ↓

Funcionalidades principais

  • Import PDF, Android-decodable images and e-invoice XML
  • Chinese OCR and PDF text-layer extraction
  • Local Room database, duplicate detection, search and categories
  • Original-file preview and CSV/full-archive export
  • User-configured WebDAV backup, verification and restore
  • Light, dark and large-screen layouts

Utilização

Archiving personal invoices, finding records, checking duplicates, exporting a copy, or explicitly backing up to WebDAV.

Privacidade e dados

Invoices may contain sensitive identity and account data. Processing and storage are local by default. Data leaves the private app directory only when you initiate WebDAV, export or sharing. WebDAV credentials are protected with Android Keystore.

Limitações

OCR and field parsing can be wrong; verify important fields manually. Some regions, layouts or encrypted PDFs may not work. WebDAV compatibility varies. This is not a tax, accounting or reimbursement-compliance service. Uninstalling may delete local data.
